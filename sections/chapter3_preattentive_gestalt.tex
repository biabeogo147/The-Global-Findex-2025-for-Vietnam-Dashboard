\section{Chapter 3: Pre-Attentive Processing, Gestalt Principles, and Magnitude Estimation}
\label{sec:chapter3-preattentive-gestalt}

\subsection{Techniques / Principles Applied}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Pre-attentive processing through contrast, saturation, and enclosure.
\item Gestalt grouping, especially proximity, similarity, and enclosure.
\item Magnitude estimation that favors common baselines and linear length for
important comparisons.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{How Applied in the Dashboard}

\subsubsection{Pre-attentive Processing}

Pre-attentive pop-out is used to direct attention before detailed reading. On
Page 1, \figref{fig:socioeconomic-matrix}{Socioeconomic matrix} uses darker
blue cells to make high-density income--education intersections stand out
quickly. On Page 2, \figref{fig:mobile-ownership-profile}{Mobile ownership profile}
differentiates smartphone and basic phone rates with strong luminance contrast,
so the penetration gap is visible without a full label-by-label scan.

\subsubsection{Gestalt Grouping}

\dashboardfigure{0.86}{fig-page3-kpi-cards.png}
{The Account Ownership \& Access Landscape KPI cards.}
{fig:account-kpi-cards}

\dashboardfigure{0.92}{fig-page3-filter-bar.png}
{Filter controls on the Account Ownership \& Access Landscape page.}
{fig:page3-filter-bar}

Gestalt grouping is especially visible on Page 3.
\figref{fig:account-kpi-cards}{Page 3 KPI cards} clusters the four KPI cards
closely together, so proximity signals that they belong to the same summary
layer. \figref{fig:page3-filter-bar}{Page 3 filter bar} adds enclosure by
separating the controls from the charts they drive, making the interaction
layer visually legible before the user reads any labels in detail.

\subsubsection{Magnitude Estimation}

Magnitude estimation is handled conservatively in the comparison-heavy charts.
\figref{fig:mobile-ownership-profile}{Mobile ownership profile} and
\figref{fig:exclusion-friction-log}{Exclusion friction log} rely on bar length
and aligned baselines because those channels are more accurate for comparing
rates than area- or angle-based alternatives.

\subsection{Notes / Adjustments}

The Account Composition Flow in
\figref{fig:account-composition-flow}{Account composition flow} is less
precise than a standard bar chart for direct magnitude comparison. The
trade-off is accepted because the page needs to emphasize relationships between
source groups and account states, not only isolated totals.
