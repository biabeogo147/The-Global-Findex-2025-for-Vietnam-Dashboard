\section{Dataset Overview}
\label{sec:dataset-overview}

The Global Findex 2025 is an appropriate dataset for this report because it
combines financial access, digital connectivity, payment behavior, saving,
borrowing, and resilience indicators in one nationally comparable survey. That
combination makes it possible to study financial inclusion as a progression
from readiness and access to usage and outcomes rather than as a single
account-ownership rate.

The dashboard analyzed in this report uses the Vietnam microdata collected
through face-to-face interviews from June 23 to August 31, 2024. The sample
covers 1,000 adults aged 15 years and older and applies survey weights so the
reported percentages represent Vietnam's adult population. The survey has an
approximate design effect of 1.33 and a margin of error of \(\pm 3.6\)
percentage points.

The report uses the dataset to answer three linked questions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{Q\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]
\item Where are the main gaps in digital readiness and formal financial access
across income, education, gender, and location?
\item When adults have accounts, do wages, transfers, sales, and merchant
payments actually move through digital channels or revert to cash?
\item How do different inclusion patterns relate to saving, borrowing, and
shock-coping strategies across socioeconomic groups?
\end{enumerate}
